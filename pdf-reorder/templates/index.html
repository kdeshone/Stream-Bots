PDF Page Reorder

Fix page order from single-sided scans of double-sided books.

How single-sided scanning works:
You scan odd pages first (1, 3, 5…), flip the stack, then scan even pages — which come out in reverse (…6, 4, 2). This tool interleaves them back into reading order.
